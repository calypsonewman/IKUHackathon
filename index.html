    Many NHS GP practices give some travel vaccines for free (hepatitis A, typhoid, cholera, and diphtheria/tetanus/polio). Others, such as yellow fever, rabies and Japanese encephalitis, are usually private and only given at travel clinics or pharmacies. Yellow fever is only given at registered centres.
