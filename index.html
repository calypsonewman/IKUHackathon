    Many NHS GP practices give some travel vaccines for free (hepatitis A, typhoid, cholera, and diphtheria/tetanus/polio). Others, such as yellow fever, rabies and Japanese encephalitis, are usually private and only given at travel clinics or pharmacies. Yellow fever is only given at registered centres.     This app gives general guidance only and is not a substitute for professional advice. Always check TravelHealthPro (NaTHNaC) and FCDO travel advice, and speak to a nurse, pharmacist or travel clinic 6–8 weeks before you travel.
